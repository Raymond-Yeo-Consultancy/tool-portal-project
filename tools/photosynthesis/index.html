Key ideas

  • Plants make their own food by photosynthesis.
  • For photosynthesis, a plant needs light, carbon dioxide and water. Chlorophyll, the green substance in the leaves, traps light energy.
  • The plant makes food (sugar) and gives off oxygen. The bubbles are oxygen.
  • More bubbles in a minute means the plant is making food faster.
carbon dioxide + water light→chlorophyll food (sugar) + oxygen

Think like a scientist

Why are there no bubbles in the dark?

Plants need light for photosynthesis. Without light the plant cannot make food, so it gives off no oxygen.

The lamp is bright but there is no carbon dioxide. What happens? Why?

There are no bubbles. Light alone is not enough. The plant also needs carbon dioxide to make food.

You want to find out how light affects photosynthesis. What must you keep the same?

Keep the carbon dioxide the same, and use the same plant in the same amount of water. Change only the light. This makes it a fair test.

What pattern do you see in your results?

The brighter the light, the more bubbles in one minute. The more carbon dioxide in the water, the more bubbles in one minute. So the plant makes food faster.

Where does the energy in the plant's food come from?

From light. During photosynthesis, light energy is converted into chemical potential energy, which is stored in the food. Animals get this energy when they eat plants.
