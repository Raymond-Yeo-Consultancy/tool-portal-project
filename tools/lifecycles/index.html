Key ideas

  • A life cycle shows the stages a living thing goes through as it grows. The adult reproduces, and the cycle starts again.
  • Some animals have a 3-stage life cycle: egg, young, adult. Examples: chicken, frog, cockroach, grasshopper.
  • Some animals have a 4-stage life cycle: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Examples: butterfly, mosquito, beetle.
  • In some life cycles the young looks like the adult. In others it looks very different.
  • A flowering plant grows from a seed into a seedling and then an adult plant.

Think like a scientist

How is a butterfly's life cycle different from a chicken's?

A butterfly has 4 stages and its young (the caterpillar) does not look like the adult. A chicken has 3 stages and the chick looks like a small adult.

A nymph and a larva are both young insects. How are they different?

A nymph looks like a small adult without wings, and there is no pupa stage. A larva looks very different from the adult and becomes a pupa before it becomes an adult.

Mosquitoes spread dengue. Which stages of the mosquito's life cycle happen in water?

The egg, larva and pupa stages. That is why we remove still water around our homes: mosquitoes cannot complete their life cycle without it.

Why is it called a life “cycle”?

A cycle goes round and round. The adult reproduces to make new young, which grow into adults and reproduce in turn. This is how each kind of living thing continues.
