Energy forms

Key ideas

  • Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be converted from one form to another.
  • When energy is converted, some of it is often converted into forms we do not want, such as heat and sound. These are shown in dashed boxes.
  • Whether a form is useful depends on the job. In a torch, heat is not wanted. In a kettle, heat is the useful form!
