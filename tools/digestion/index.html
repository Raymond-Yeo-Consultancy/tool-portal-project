Key ideas

  • Digestion breaks food down into simpler substances that the body can use.
  • Food travels through the mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine and large intestine, and undigested food leaves through the anus.
  • Digestion takes place in the mouth, the stomach and the small intestine. It is completed in the small intestine.
  • Digested food passes into the blood in the small intestine. The blood carries it to all parts of the body.
  • The organs work together as the digestive system.

Think like a scientist

Why should you chew your food well?

Chewing breaks food into smaller pieces. Digestive juices can then mix with it and digest it more easily.

Where does digestion start, and where is it completed?

It starts in the mouth and is completed in the small intestine.

What does the gullet do? Is food digested there?

The gullet only moves food from the mouth to the stomach. No digestion takes place in the gullet.

How does digested food reach your muscles and your brain?

In the small intestine, digested food passes into the blood. The blood carries it to every part of the body.

What happens in the large intestine?

There is no digestion here. Water is taken in from the undigested food, and the solid waste that is left passes out through the anus.
