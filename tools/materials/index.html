Key ideas

  • Different materials have different properties.
  • A strong material does not break easily.
  • A flexible material bends easily. A stiff material does not.
  • A waterproof material does not let water pass through or soak in.
  • Some materials let light pass through, so we can see through them.
  • We choose a material for an object because of its properties.

Think like a scientist

Why is a window made of glass and not wood?

Glass lets light pass through, so we can see through it and the room is bright. Wood does not let light through.

Glass and metal are both stiff and waterproof. How are they different?

Glass breaks easily and lets light through. Metal is strong and does not let light through.

Why is a towel made of cotton fabric and not plastic?

Cotton fabric soaks up water, so it dries you. Plastic is waterproof: the water would stay on your skin.

To make these tests fair, what must be the same for every material?

The strips should be the same size and thickness, and each test must be done in the same way: the same hammer, the same weight, the same amount of water and the same torch.
