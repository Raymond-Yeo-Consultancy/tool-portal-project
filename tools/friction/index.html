Think like a scientist

You want to find out how the type of surface affects the distance the car travels.

Which things must you keep the same?

Keep the ramp height the same and use the same car. Change only the surface. This makes it a fair test.

Which surface gives the most friction?

Sandpaper. It is the roughest surface, so it has the most friction and the car stops soonest. Glass is the smoothest, so the car travels the furthest.

Key idea

Friction is a force that opposes motion between two surfaces in contact.

The rougher the surface, the greater the friction, and the shorter the distance the car moves. A higher ramp gives the car more energy, so it travels further.

The red arrow shows friction acting on the moving car. It points opposite to the direction the car is moving, so the car slows down and stops.
