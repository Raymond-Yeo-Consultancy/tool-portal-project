Key ideas

  • At the highest points, the bob has the most gravitational potential energy and no kinetic energy.
  • At the lowest point, the bob moves fastest. It has the most kinetic energy.
  • As the bob swings, energy is converted from gravitational potential energy to kinetic energy and back again.
  • Energy is not created or destroyed. With air resistance, some energy is converted into heat energy, so each swing is a little lower.

Think like a scientist

Where does the bob have the most kinetic energy? How can you tell?

At the lowest point. The bob is moving fastest there. Press Stop at lowest point and look at the bars.

Without air resistance, what do you notice about the Total energy bar?

It stays the same length all the time. Energy is only converted from one form to another. The bob swings back up to its release height every time.

With air resistance, why do the swings get smaller?

Air resistance is a frictional force that opposes the bob's motion. Some of the bob's energy is converted into heat energy, so there is less energy left to carry the bob up. In the end the pendulum stops.

What changes the time for one swing: the release height or the string length?

The string length. A longer string gives a longer time for one swing. Changing the release height hardly changes the time at all. Check your results table!

A higher release gives more energy. Why?

The higher an object is above the ground, the more gravitational potential energy it has. Release the bob from 15 cm and its bars are longer than from 5 cm.
