Think like a scientist

The water looks “more” in the tall cylinder than in the wide dish. Is it?

No. The water level is higher because the cylinder is narrow, but the volume is still 100 cm³. A liquid changes shape, not volume.

Sand can be poured like water. Why is it a solid?

Look at one grain: it keeps its own shape. Sand is made of many tiny solids. A pile of sand does not become flat like water does.

Why can a lot of air be pumped into a bicycle tyre?

Air is a gas, and a gas can be compressed. More and more air is squeezed into the same space inside the tyre.

An “empty” bottle is pushed upside down into water. Why does water not fill it?

The bottle is not empty: it is full of air. Air is matter and occupies space, so the water cannot get in unless the air gets out.
