Key ideas

  • Light travels in straight lines.
  • A shadow forms when an object blocks light.
  • The nearer the object is to the light source, the bigger its shadow.
  • Opaque objects do not let light through. Translucent objects let some light through. Transparent objects let most light through.

Think like a scientist

Why does the shadow get bigger when the disc moves towards the torch?

Light spreads out from the torch in straight lines. Near the torch, the disc blocks a wider part of the light, so the dark area on the screen is bigger.

Why is the shadow the same shape as the disc?

Light travels in straight lines and cannot bend round the disc. The light is blocked only where the disc is, so the shadow has the outline of the disc.

How could you make the shadow bigger without moving the disc?

Move the torch closer to the disc, or move the screen further away.

To test how distance affects the shadow, what must you keep the same?

The same disc (size and material), the same torch and the same screen position. Change only the distance between the disc and the torch.
