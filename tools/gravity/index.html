Key ideas

  • Gravitational force is the pull of the Earth on objects. It pulls every object towards the Earth, so things fall when we let go.
  • Air resistance is a frictional force. It opposes the motion of objects moving through air.
  • The larger the surface area facing the air, the greater the air resistance, and the more slowly the object falls. A light object with a large surface area, like a feather, is slowed the most.
  • With no air, there is no air resistance. All the objects fall together and land at the same time!

Think like a scientist

The flat sheet and the crumpled ball are made from the same paper. Why did they land at different times?

They have the same mass, so the gravitational force on them is the same. The flat sheet has a larger surface area, so it has more air resistance and falls more slowly.

Why is comparing the flat sheet and the crumpled ball a fair test?

Only one thing is different: the shape (surface area). The mass, the material and the drop height are all the same.

Look at the dots. What do they tell you?

Each dot shows where the object was every 0.1 s. For the metal ball, the dots get further apart: it is speeding up as it falls. For the feather, the dots are soon evenly spaced: air resistance has become as large as the gravitational force, so the feather falls at a steady speed.

What happened with no air? Why?

All four objects landed at the same time. Without air there is no air resistance, so gravitational force is the only force acting on them. An astronaut tried this on the Moon (which has no air) with a hammer and a feather. They landed together!

How does a parachute use this idea?

A parachute has a very large surface area. This gives a lot of air resistance, which slows the fall so the person lands safely.
