Think like a scientist

How can you tell a plant cell from an animal cell under a microscope?

A plant cell has a cell wall, so it has a regular shape, and it may have green chloroplasts. An animal cell has neither, and its shape is irregular.

Why do animal cells not need chloroplasts?

Chloroplasts are for making food from light. Animals do not make their own food. They get it by eating plants or other animals.

A root cell is a plant cell, but it has no chloroplasts. Why?

Roots grow underground where there is no light, so chloroplasts would be of no use there. Not every plant cell has chloroplasts.

What would happen to a cell without a nucleus?

The nucleus controls all the cell's activities. Without it, the cell could not carry out its activities properly and would die.
