Key ideas

  • A food chain shows the food relationship between organisms. Each arrow means “is eaten by”.
  • A food chain starts with a producer. Plants are producers: they make their own food by photosynthesis.
  • Animals are consumers. They get their energy by eating other organisms.
  • A predator hunts and eats other animals. The animals it eats are its prey.
  • A food web is made up of food chains that are linked together.
  • When the population of one organism changes, other organisms in the food web are affected.

Think like a scientist

Where does the energy in a food chain come from?

From the Sun. Plants trap light energy to make food. The energy is passed on when an animal eats the plant, and again when another animal eats that animal.

Why does every food chain start with a plant?

Only plants can make their own food. Animals cannot, so they depend on plants directly (by eating them) or indirectly (by eating animals that eat plants).

Can an animal be a predator and a prey?

Yes. In the garden, the frog is a predator of grasshoppers and snails, and it is prey for the snake.

In the garden, what happens at first if all the frogs are removed?

The snakes decrease, because they have nothing to eat. The grasshoppers and snails increase, because fewer are eaten. Try it in “What if?”.

Why is a food web a better picture of a habitat than one food chain?

Most animals eat more than one kind of food. A food web shows all these links, so we can see how a change to one organism can affect many others.
