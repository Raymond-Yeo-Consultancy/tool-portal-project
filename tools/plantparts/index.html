Plant Parts

A plant has different parts, and each part has a job to do. Together they keep the plant alive.

Key ideas

  • Roots take in water and mineral salts, and hold the plant firmly in the soil.
  • The stem holds the plant upright and carries water from the roots to the other parts.
  • Leaves make food for the plant.
  • Flowers help the plant to reproduce.
  • The parts work together as a system. If one part cannot do its job, the whole plant is affected.

Think like a scientist

What would happen to a plant if its roots were cut off?

It could not take in water, so it would wilt and die. It would also fall over, because nothing would hold it in the soil.

A white flower is left standing in blue-coloured water. After a day its petals turn blue. Why?

The stem carried the coloured water up to the flower. This shows that water travels through the stem to the other parts of the plant.

Why do most leaves grow where they can catch sunlight?

Leaves need sunlight to make food. The stem holds them up and spreads them out so that they get as much light as possible.

All the leaves are eaten by caterpillars. Why does the plant stop growing?

Without leaves, the plant cannot make food. With no food, it cannot grow.
